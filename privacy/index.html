Privacy Policy

Hungry Pug: Snake Game

Effective November 5, 2026

Who we are

Hungry Pug: Snake Game is made by GW Consulting Company INC, shown as GWC on the App Store. In this policy, "we" means that company. You don't make an account with us to play.

Questions or requests: clark@gwccompany.com

Challenges

Challenges are saved on Apple's iCloud servers, in a public database, so another phone can open them.

  • If you send a challenge, we save your Game Center display name (or "A Hungry Pug player" if you aren't signed in to Game Center), your score, your pug and difficulty, the board, and a recording of your run. Anyone with the link can see these and watch the run.
  • If you play a challenge, we save your Game Center display name (or the same stand-in), your score and whether you won, and whether you finished the run. The person who sent the challenge sees this in the game. The challenge screen tells you this before you play.
  • iCloud also attaches an ID to each saved record, created by Apple, so that only you can change your record. Saving a score needs you to be signed in to iCloud.

We keep this information so challenges and results keep working, until you ask us to delete it.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's software may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the game asks), your IP address and the rough location it suggests, how you interact with ads and the game, and diagnostic and crash information. It uses this to show ads and measure how they do.

You can change your tracking choice any time in iPhone Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Google explains how it handles this information in the Google Privacy Policy.

Apple services

  • Game Center: if you're signed in, your scores go to the game's leaderboards, handled by Apple.
  • Purchases: Apple handles Remove Ads. We never see your payment details.

Apple explains how it handles this information in the Apple Privacy Policy.

On your device

Your scores, unlocked dogs and settings are stored on your device. Deleting the game can remove them.

Your choices

To have your challenges and results deleted, email us and include a challenge link or the name shown in the game, so we can find them. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over your information, such as seeing a copy of it. Email us and we'll help.

Deleting the game stops it collecting anything further.

Children

The game isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child under 13 has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
