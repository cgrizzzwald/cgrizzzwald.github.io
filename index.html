Hungry Pug Game

A snake game starring a very hungry pug. Eat bones, skip the grapes, and try not to run into your own trail.

Download on the App Store

Support

Something broken, an idea, or a purchase that did not go through? Email clark@gwccompany.com and you will get a reply from a real person.

It helps to include your device model, your iOS version, and what happened right before the problem.

Common questions

  • I bought Remove Ads and the ads came back. Tap RESTORE on the main menu. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they carry across your devices.
  • How do I unlock the other dogs? Keep playing. Each one has its own challenge, shown on its card in Characters.
  • The game is silent. Check the ring/silent switch on the side of your iPhone.
