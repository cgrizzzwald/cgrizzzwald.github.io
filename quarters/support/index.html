Support

Quarters: Pitching Pennies

Get in touch

Need help or found a bug? Email clark@gwccompany.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We read everything.

How do I play?

Open How to Play on the home screen. Drag to aim and set power, then tap to time the release. In Pitch, closest to the wall wins, and a coin leaning on the wall beats everything. In Lag, closest to the line without going over wins.

Who am I playing?

In Play you face street rivals: computer players built to throw like real people. To play a real person, sign in to Game Center and tap Challenge a Friend. You each throw once, whenever you like, within 7 days.

What happens to my coin when I lose?

Every game is played for the coin you throw. Win and you keep yours and take theirs; lose and yours goes to the winner. Practice costs nothing.

I ran out of coins

You get free Quarters every day, and the Free Coin Drop on the home screen refills on a timer.

My coins are gone after reinstalling

Progress is saved to your iCloud. Check that you're signed in to iCloud (the game's Settings shows "iCloud backup: on"), open the game, and return to the home screen once.

I bought a coin pack and didn't get it

Close and reopen the game. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with the date and the pack. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Are the ads required?

No. An ad plays only if you tap Watch an ad after a win, to double the coins you won. Legendary coins can't be doubled.

Can I sell, trade or cash out coins?

No. Coins have no cash value and stay in the game.
