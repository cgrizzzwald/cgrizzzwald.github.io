Privacy Policy

Quarters: Pitching Pennies

Effective November 24, 2026

Who we are

Quarters: Pitching Pennies is made by GW Consulting Company INC, shown as GWC on the App Store. In this policy, "we" means that company. You don't make an account with us to play, and we run no servers for this game: we don't receive, store or sell information about you.

Questions or requests: clark@gwccompany.com

On your device

Your coins, wins, streaks, settings, daily challenges and collection are saved on your device.

Apple services

  • iCloud: if you're signed in to iCloud, your game progress is also saved to your own iCloud account, so it comes back after a reinstall or on another of your devices. Apple stores it under your Apple Account. We can't see it. You can turn this off in iPhone Settings, under your name, then iCloud.
  • Game Center: if you're signed in, your longest win streak goes to the game's leaderboard, and you can challenge your Game Center friends. Your Game Center name is shown to the friends you play and on the leaderboard. Friend games (the throws and the result) are stored by Apple's Game Center. You can play without Game Center.
  • Purchases: Apple handles coin packs. We never see your payment details. The game keeps a record on your device, and in your iCloud save, of which packs were delivered.

Apple explains how it handles this information in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Ads

Ads are optional: one plays only if you tap to watch it after a win. They come from Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google's software in the game may collect your IP address and the rough location it suggests, a device identifier, which ads you saw and how you interacted with them, how you use the game, and diagnostic, performance and crash information. Google uses this to show ads and measure how they do.

The game does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and it does not use your device's advertising identifier.

Where the law requires your consent for ads, the game asks you before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice any time in the game under Settings, then Ad privacy choices.

Google explains how it handles this information in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

Deleting the game removes what is on your device. To remove the iCloud copy, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, then Quarters. To leave Game Center, open iPhone Settings, then Game Center.

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over your information. Email us and we'll help.

Children

The game isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
