Common questions

  • How do I fly? Use the left side of the screen. Slide up to flap, let go to glide, slide down and hold to dive. Flap out of a dive to swoop up with extra speed.
  • Why can't I poop? Your Poop Power tank is empty. Swoop down and steal a snack from someone on the boardwalk to refill it.
  • I bought "No Ads + Free Revives" and it's not working. Tap Restore Purchase on the main menu. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they carry across your devices.
  • How do I unlock gull skins? Every metre you fly adds to your Journey. Each waypoint you reach on the map unlocks a new skin. Skins change your look only, not your score.
  • The game is silent. Check the ring/silent switch on the side of your iPhone, and the sound setting on the main menu.
