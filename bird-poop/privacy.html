Bird Poop! Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information the Bird Poop! Seagull Arcade iPhone app ("the app") collects and how it is used. The app is published by GW Consulting Company INC ("we", "us").

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or password.
  • We do not run our own servers and do not receive your game data.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Personalized ads are only shown if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

Information stored on your device

Your high score, Journey distance, unlocked gull skins, daily challenge progress and settings (sound, vibration) are saved only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads, including an optional ad you can watch to revive during a run. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in those ads, Google may collect:

  • Device information such as model, operating system version and IP address (which may indicate approximate location)
  • The Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • Ad interactions, such as ads viewed and tapped
  • Diagnostic information such as crash and performance data from the ad SDK

The app asks for tracking permission with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt after you have played a few rounds. If you decline, Google does not receive the IDFA and you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchase

The optional "No Ads + Free Revives" purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase was made, so it can remove ads and restore the purchase on your other devices.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your score is submitted to the Bird Poop! leaderboard, where other players can see it alongside your Game Center nickname. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center in iPhone Settings.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app creates an image of your score and opens the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose where to share it.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app has no accounts, chat or other way for players to submit personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Decline or turn off tracking in iPhone Settings to stop personalized ads.
  • Buy "No Ads + Free Revives" to stop non-optional ads.
  • Delete the app to remove all game data stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
